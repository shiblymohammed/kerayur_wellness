# Kerayur Wellness & Retreats - Master Homepage Blueprint

## 1. The Strategy: "Selling the Destination to Sell the Cure"
International clients are looking for an escape, an adventure, and a transformation. We are not just selling a medical treatment; we are selling the magic of Kerala. This homepage will serve as an interactive, highly immersive storybook. We will use modern web technologies (WebGL, Scroll-Triggered Animations, Parallax, and Video Masking) to create a premium, visually stunning experience that commands high-ticket pricing.

---

## 2. Homepage Sections (The Narrative Arc)

### Section 1: **"The Awakening"** (Hero Section)
*   **Unique Visual:** The page loads in pitch black with a single glowing, golden drop of Ayurvedic oil in the center. As the user scrolls down, the drop ripples out (WebGL water effect), breaking the darkness to reveal a breathtaking, full-screen, 4K aerial drone video of Kerala (misty mountains, backwaters, ocean).
*   **Content:**
    *   *Headline:* "Awaken in God's Own Country."
    *   *Subtext:* "A sanctuary where ancient healing meets untouched nature. Begin your transformation in Kerala."
    *   *Interaction:* A custom cursor that acts like a soft glowing light, illuminating the text.

### Section 2: **"The Canvas"** (Landscapes & Geography)
*   **Unique Visual (The Map Cutout Scroll-Reveal):** As the user transitions from the hero, a solid background emerges with a massive cutout in the shape of the **Kerala map**. Inside the map cutout, a preview of Kerala's dynamic landscapes is visible. As the user scrolls, the cutout scales up (zooms in) massively until the map borders push entirely outside the screen. Once "inside," it opens up into a horizontal scroll journey across three distinct environments.
*   **Content:**
    *   *The Blue (Backwaters):* Smooth scrolling, water reflection effects. "Float through the veins of the earth in Alleppey."
    *   *The Green (Munnar):* Parallax scrolling through layers of tea leaves. "Breathe the pure prana (life force) of the Western Ghats."
    *   *The Gold (Nattika Beach):* Golden sand textures and rolling waves. "Ground yourself where the ocean meets the soul."

### Section 4: **"The Roots"** (Culture, Tradition & Art)
*   **Unique Visual:** A dynamic split-screen layout. 
    *   **Left Side (North Kerala):** Deep reds and fiery oranges. A slow-motion video of **Theyyam**. Text: "The fiery energy of the North."
    *   **Right Side (South Kerala):** Rich greens and golds. A slow-motion video of a **Kathakali** dancer's eyes and expressions. Text: "The disciplined wisdom of the South."

### Section 5: **"The Alchemy"** (The Science of Ayurveda)
*   **Unique Visual:** Massive, bold typography that says "AYURVEDA". The text itself acts as a video mask—inside the letters, you see macro shots of herbal powders being mixed, warm oils being poured, and gentle massages. 
*   **Interaction:** As the user scrolls, the text scales up infinitely until the video inside the letter 'A' takes over the entire screen, transitioning into the next section.
*   **Content:** "Born from the earth, perfected over 5,000 years. This is not a spa. This is the medical science of life."
